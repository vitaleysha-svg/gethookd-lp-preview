What Meta will ask of usYou'll know which of these are already done

  • Permission: ads_read only. We never need ads_management (write). Page and Instagram permissions only if we want post-based ads and video files, and each one adds App Review work.
  • Advanced Access to ads_read for accounts owned by other businesses. Standard Access only works for people with a role on our app. Adding clients as app Testers to skip review breaks Meta's rules.
  • Business Verification of the business that owns the app, then Tech Provider access verification (Meta says about 5 days). Until it passes, calls can fail with error 100 "Object with ID does not exist", which looks like a bad ID and is easy to misread.
  • App Review: a written use case and a screencast per permission, showing a client clicking Connect, picking the ad account, and our screen using the data.
  • Marketing API tier (Limited or Full) is separate from the permission level. Limited is "for development only" with one system user. Full now needs 500 calls in 15 days (lowered in May 2026). For one client, rate limits are not the problem.
  • Login: Meta prefers Facebook Login for Business with a saved configuration. A business integration token avoids the 60-day expiry of personal tokens. The person who connects needs a role on the ad account, and on the Page if we want video files.

Things to watch

  • Version deadline: Marketing API v24 stops working on 6 Oct 2026, 12 days from now. v23 already stopped in June. If the old Creative Analyzer code is on v23 or v24, it's already dead or will be in 12 days. Build on v25 or later (the changelog lists v26 as current).
  • Attribution: since 12 Jan 2026 Meta no longer returns 7-day view and 28-day view windows. Our ROAS may not match what a client sees if they use view windows.
  • What ran is not always what is stored: Advantage+ text tweaks, auto crops and catalog templates mean the stored copy can differ from what people saw.
  • Client data rules: Meta's terms say a connected client's ads and results can only be used for that client. They must not go into the shared ad library or anyone else's benchmarks, and must be deleted when the client asks or leaves. Stored creatives should be covered in the pilot contract.
