What the $299 includesTap any item for the detail. This is the scope for the contract.

1Onboarding call60 min

60 minutes, once. They walk us through what they sell, who buys it and what they have tried. We look at their last 30 days together, name the one thing holding the account back, and agree the number the pilot is judged on and what good looks like in 30 days. They leave with the export instructions and the Slack invite.

2First analysis, the written game planonce

Back 1 to 2 business days after they send their numbers. Every ad is marked as a hook problem, a body problem, an offer or page problem, or fine. We count how many genuinely different concepts they have tested, check which buyer awareness levels they reach and miss, and add the market side: winners in their niche, what competitors launched recently, angles nobody in their market runs. Every recommended move names the live ad behind it.

3Weekly readweekly

They send one export a week and get the read back in 1 to 2 business days, one page, in their Slack channel. It covers the one thing to do this week, what to kill (with the number), what to feed (with the spend), what to test next (the angle, the live ad that proves it, its link, how long it has run), where their creative sits against the market, and what changed on the agreed number.

4Three static ad concepts a week, built off a proven winner3 a week

With each weekly read. We take a winning ad from their market and build three static versions for their product with Clone Ads, using their logo, product photos and brand colours. Each comes with the source ad. Static only.

5Two strategy calls a month2 x 30 min

30 minutes each, with the strategist who wrote the read. Go through the read, look at anything launched since the last call, decide what gets tested next.

6Private Slack channelsame day

Same-day answers between calls: send a creative before launch for a straight read, ask for a competitor or market pull, flag a number that moved.

7Monthly open webinarmonthly

Once a month a creative strategist takes live questions from the whole group.

8GetHookd software, includedincluded

GetHookd comes with the $299, with API and MCP access turned on so they can connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or Perplexity. No separate subscription. The prompt library comes with it.

9Intake form and export instructionssetup

About ten minutes for the form. The Ads Manager export takes about five minutes the first time and under a minute after that with a saved preset.

Not included

  • Video ads and UGC, and anything that needs a shoot. Static concepts only.
  • Access to their ad account. For now they send an export. (The Facebook connection is in the other doc; leave it out of the contract until it works.)
  • More than two 30-minute calls a month.
  • Refunds.

What the client sends us

  • The signed contract and the paid invoice.
  • The 60-minute onboarding call, then they join their private Slack channel.
  • The intake form: what they sell and at what margin, target cost per purchase and ROAS, what happened in the last 30 days, logo, product photos, brand colours, anything they can't claim, who approves tests, and what has to happen in 30 days for this to be worth it.
  • 3 to 5 competitors they watch.
  • An Ads Manager export every week, plus the creative for their top 10 ads by spend and their 5 worst.

Two things worth a line in the contract

  • Calls. The offer promises two 30-minute calls a month. Alex wants calls to drop over time as more of this gets automated, so you may want the contract to say "up to two".
  • Webinar. It's promised monthly, but nobody has been named to run it yet.
