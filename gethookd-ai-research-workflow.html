Connecting GetHookd to your AI agent is the easy part. The reason most people connect it and then stall is that they type random questions, get a mediocre answer, and decide the AI thing is overrated. It isn't. They just never ran it in the right order.

This is the order. Same three steps, every session.

Before you start

Have GetHookd connected to your AI agent (Claude or ChatGPT) and know the one thing you need going in: your niche, or your product. That's it. Swap the bracketed parts in each prompt for yours.

STEP 1

Find the winners worth your time

Surface the ads that have already proven themselves. Run time is the whole game: an ad that has been live for months is paying for itself, or it would have been switched off.

Paste this
Using GetHookd, pull the top winning image ads in [your niche], running 45+ days, sorted by longest run time. For the top 5, give me the brand, how many days it has been live, the hook, and the one angle I should steal. Longest-running first.
How to read what comes back
  • Run time is your #1 filter. Something live 300+ days is a proven money-maker. Ignore the flashy new ones, they haven't earned it yet.
  • Hunt for the repeated angle. If the same message shows up across three or four winners, that's not a coincidence, it's the market telling you what it buys.
  • Throw out the noise: page-like ads, generic marketplace/dropship reposts, anything under 45 days.
Your decision: pick the ONE angle that shows up most and fits your product. You now know what the market already responds to.
STEP 2

Decode why the leader keeps winning

One winning ad is luck you can't read. The pattern behind a brand running dozens of them is a playbook you can. Go deep on the #1 brand from Step 1.

Paste this
Take the #1 brand from that list and break down everything they're running in GetHookd: how many active ads, the angles they repeat most, their single longest-running ad, and the structure of that winning ad (the hook, the body, the offer, the call to action). I want the pattern behind why they win, not just one creative.
How to read what comes back
  • Active ad count = confidence. Brands multiply what works. A brand running 40 versions isn't lazy, it's telling you the concept converts.
  • The angle they repeat most is the proven message. Their longest-runner is the proven creative. You want both.
  • Map the skeleton: the hook (what stops the scroll), the agitation and proof (why you should care), the offer (what makes it a yes), the CTA. That skeleton is the part you keep.
Your decision: you now hold the proven angle AND the proven structure. You are no longer guessing what a good ad looks like in your niche.
STEP 3

Clone it around your own product

This is not copying. You are applying a proven structure to your product, your brand, your offer. The idea is borrowed, everything the customer sees is yours.

Paste this
Take the longest-running winner and rebuild it around my product with Clone Ads. My product is [product + the one main benefit]. Keep the proven hook structure and offer framing, swap in my product, and give me launch-ready ad copy plus 2 hook variations to test.
How to use what comes back
  • Keep the structure, change the specifics. Same skeleton, your product, your proof, your offer. If you rewrite the structure you've thrown away the only thing that was proven.
  • The 2 hook variations are your first test. Same body, two different opening lines. Let the market pick the winner instead of you guessing.
  • Compliance pass. In a policed niche (health, finance, supplements) keep claims clean, lead with the outcome, put the specifics on the page, not in the ad.
Your decision: you have launch-ready copy and a first test, built on something that already works. Ship it.

The one-sitting run

  1. Step 1 prompt → pick the angle that repeats.
  2. Step 2 prompt → pull the leader's structure.
  3. Step 3 prompt → get the copy plus 2 hooks.
  4. Drop it into your ad account and launch the test.

Ten to fifteen minutes, and you did it without opening a single tab of the dashboard. That's the difference between "I connected the AI" and "the AI runs my research for me."

The four ways people get this wrong

  • Chasing new over proven. The shiny ad from last week hasn't survived anything. Run time is the truth serum.
  • Copying the product, not the structure. You don't want their product, you want the reason people buy it.
  • Skipping Step 2. Cloning one random creative without decoding the pattern is how you copy a brand that's quietly dying.
  • Testing ten things at once. Two hooks, same body. One clean variable. Anything more and you learn nothing.

Bonus: 5 prompts to keep on hand

Scan a niche fast
Using GetHookd, show me the top 10 longest-running winning image ads in [niche] with the hook and the angle for each.
Spy one competitor
Break down [brand] in GetHookd: every active angle they're testing, their longest-running ad, and what they've stopped running.
Steal the hooks
Give me the opening line of the 10 longest-running winners in [niche], side by side, and tell me the pattern.
Find the growing stores
In [niche], which stores are growing (traffic climbing), how many products they carry, and what they're advertising most.
Turn a winner into copy
Rebuild the longest-running [niche] winner around my product [product], keep the structure, give me 3 hook options.

Run it once. The click everyone talks about happens on the first pass, not the tenth. If you get stuck anywhere, just reply and tell me where.
