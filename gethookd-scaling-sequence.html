STEP 0 · THE GATE

Work out where you actually are

Most people restart from scratch when they are already three steps in. Read this and jump.
No niche yet or you keep changing it  →  Step 1
Niche picked, no product  →  Step 2
Product picked, not sure it is real  →  Step 3
Product is real, no creative  →  Step 4
Running ads, they are not converting  →  Step 5
One ad works, need more  →  Step 6
STEP 1

Pick the niche on evidence, not on what you like

A niche with 40 brands running 30+ active ads each has money moving through it. One with 3 brands does not.
You have the GetHookd MCP connected. I need to pick an ecommerce niche
to build a store in.

Do this:
1. List the available niches.
2. For each niche, aggregate the ads to get: how many distinct brands are
   currently advertising, the total count of ACTIVE ads, and the median
   number of active ads per brand.
3. Drop any niche where fewer than 20 brands are actively advertising.
4. Rank what is left by median active ads per brand, highest first. That
   number tells me who is spending, not just who is present.

Give me the top 5 as a table: niche, brands advertising, total active ads,
median active ads per brand.

Then for each of the 5, tell me in one line what kind of buyer it is and
what the average product price looks like based on the shops in it.

Do not give me your opinion on which is best until I ask.
You now have: 5 niches ranked by real advertiser spend. Pick one. Write it down, you will paste it into every prompt after this.
DO IT IN THE APP: Explore, open the niche filter, and set Active ads only. Note the result count for each niche you are considering. Then open Shops and see how many distinct brands appear. High ad count plus many brands equals a real market.
STEP 2

Find what is actually winning in it

This is the filter recipe. It is the single most useful thing in this document.
Niche: [PASTE YOUR NICHE FROM STEP 1]

Search the ad database with these filters, all of them:
- 4 or 5 star performance rating
- ACTIVE ads only (an inactive ad is a failed test, it teaches me nothing)
- running 15 days or longer
- the advertising brand has 10 or more active ads
- ad rank in the top 5 percent

Return the top 30 results.

Then group them by the product being sold and give me a table:
product, how many different brands are running ads for it, total active
ads across those brands, the longest runtime on any single ad.

Sort by longest runtime, descending.

Why runtime: nobody keeps paying to run an ad that loses money. A 60 day
ad is a profitable ad. Treat runtime as the signal, not the creative.
You now have: a shortlist of products that real operators are spending real money on today.
DO IT IN THE APP: Explore, then set the filters in this order: 4-5 stars, Active only, runtime 15+ days, 10+ active ads per brand, ad rank top 5%. That takes roughly 11 million ads down to a few dozen. Sort by runtime.
STEP 3

The kill test

Most shortlists are full of things that were hot three weeks ago. This kills them.
Here is my product shortlist from step 2:
[PASTE THE TABLE]

For each product, run these four checks and give me a PASS or FAIL on each:

1. SCALE: is at least one brand running 25 or more active ads on it?
   A brand running 3 ads is testing. A brand running 40 is scaling.
2. STAYING POWER: is the longest running ad 30 days or more?
3. NOT A ONE HIT: are at least 2 different brands selling it?
   One brand means one lucky store. Two or more means a market.
4. STILL CLIMBING: pull the ads-launched-over-time trend for the top
   brand. Is the count of new ads flat or rising over the last 30 days,
   or is it falling off?

Kill anything that fails 2 or more.

Then rank the survivors and tell me, in one sentence each, why this
product is being bought. Not what it is. Why someone pays for it.
You now have: 2 or 3 products that survive scrutiny. Pick one.
DO IT IN THE APP: Brand Spy the top brand for each product. The panels give you active ad count, ads launched over time, and longest runtime. Falling launch trend means the winner is already cooling.
STEP 4

Pull apart the person already winning

Stop inventing. Somebody has already paid to find out what works here.
Product: [YOUR PICK FROM STEP 3]
Top competitor brand: [THE BRAND RUNNING THE MOST ACTIVE ADS ON IT]

Run a full brand spy on them and give me:

1. FORMAT MIX: what percent of their active ads are image, video, carousel?
   Where is their money actually going?
2. TOP 10 BY PERFORMANCE: their ten best ads, with runtime on each.
3. HOOKS: the opening line of each of those 10. List them verbatim.
4. LANDING PAGES: which pages the top ads point at, and how many ads
   point at each. The page with the most ads behind it is the one converting.
5. LAUNCH PATTERN: when do they drop new creative? Weekly? In batches?

Then tell me the one thing they do in almost every top ad that they do
not do in their weak ones.
You now have: their playbook, in their own numbers.
DO IT IN THE APP: Brand Spy, type the brand name. Format split, ads over time, landing pages, top hooks and top 10 by performance are all panels on that page. If the brand is missing, import it with the Chrome extension and it stays in permanently.
STEP 5

Extract the angle

The product is not why it sells. The angle is. This is the step people skip and then wonder why their clone flopped.
Here are the top 10 ads from step 4: [PASTE]

Transcribe the video ads. For the images, read the on-image copy.

Then, across all 10, pull out:

1. THE PROMISE: what specific outcome is being sold? Use their words.
2. THE MECHANISM: what reason do they give for why it works? The
   "because" that makes the promise believable.
3. THE PROOF: what makes it credible? Demo, before-after, a number,
   a person, a credential?
4. THE ENEMY: what do they position against? A competing product, a
   habit, an old way of doing it?
5. THE OPENING 3 SECONDS: what pattern do the hooks share?

Give me the 3 angles that appear most often, ranked by how many of the
top 10 use each one.

Then tell me one angle in this niche that NOBODY in these 10 is using,
and whether there is a reason nobody is using it.
You now have: the three angles proven in this niche, plus one gap.
DO IT IN THE APP: Open each top ad and use the transcription. Save all 10 to a board so the set stays together.
STEP 6

Make your version

Their proven structure, your product in it.
Take the single best performing ad from step 4 (longest runtime).

Clone it with my product:
Product URL: [YOUR PRODUCT PAGE]

Keep: the layout, the composition, the hook structure, the angle we
identified as most used in step 5.
Change: the product, the brand marks, the colours to mine.

Then make 3 variations, each one using a DIFFERENT angle from step 5,
so I am testing angles rather than testing colours.

For each variation, write the primary text and the headline in the same
voice as the original ad. Not my voice. The voice that is already working
in this niche.
You now have: four finished ads. One proven structure, three angle tests.
DO IT IN THE APP: Clone Ads. Paste your product URL, pick the winning ad as the base, generate. Load your real product shots into Brand Assets first or you will get an AI approximation of your packaging instead of your packaging.
STEP 7

Script and landing page

The ad gets the click. This is what happens after it.
Using the winning angle from step 5 and the landing pages from step 4:

1. Write a 30 second video script in this structure:
   hook (3s) / problem (5s) / mechanism (8s) / proof (8s) / offer and CTA (6s)
   Match the pacing of the transcribed winners, not a generic ad script.

2. Give me the landing page structure the top competitor uses: what is
   above the fold, what order the sections run in, where the buy button
   repeats, what objections they handle and in what order.

3. Tell me the 3 things their page does that most stores in this niche
   do not do.

Write the script so it can be filmed on a phone with no actors.
You now have: a script you can shoot today and a page structure copied from something already converting.

The loop after step 7

When an ad fatigues, do not start at step 1. Go back to step 5 and run a different angle through step 6. Your product and your competitor research stay valid for months. Only the angle wears out. Most people restart the whole process and lose weeks.

The one rule that matters more than any prompt here. Filter to ACTIVE ads only, always. An inactive ad is a test that failed and somebody stopped paying for it. Studying inactive ads is studying losers. Every filter in this document has active-only baked in for that reason.
