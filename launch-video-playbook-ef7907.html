The GetHookd 4.0 launch video, start to finish

24 September 2026. How we made the 4.0 video with Claude Code, HyperFrames and Higgsfield, written so anyone can repeat it. The script is in the middle of this page. Private until launch: these features aren't announced yet.

You approve, Claude builds. A 60-second video takes an afternoon.

How it works: Claude writes the script. A second Claude terminal builds it. Higgsfield makes the filmed-looking shots, HyperFrames edits and renders. Our first cut rendered in 2 min 38 s and used 336 Higgsfield credits.

Mladen changed the script: the video introduces what's new in 4.0, not GetHookd. That's version 2, below.

The better way to get app footage: Claude opens its own browser window and captures GetHookd itself. You log in once. Step 5.

Set up onceYou already have Claude Code, Higgsfield, Node and ffmpeg, so HyperFrames is the only install.

1Add the HyperFrames skillsYou
npx skills add heygen-com/hyperframesWhyThis teaches Claude to edit and render video. HyperFrames is HeyGen's free "After Effects, written as code".

Optional for unannounced launches: npx hyperframes telemetry disable turns off HyperFrames' anonymous usage stats. The skill sources are recorded in ~/.agents/.skill-lock.json on this Mac.

Make the videoSteps 2 to 10. Pills show who does it.

2Give Claude the briefYou
Say what's launching, who it's for, where it posts (X) and how long (60 seconds). Share old cuts as reference only.WhyThe brief decides the story. A vague brief gets a generic video.
3Approve the scriptYou
Read it (below). Each beat is one voiceover line plus one visual, tagged Higgsfield or HyperFrames.WhyThe build follows the script word for word, so this is the cheapest place to change anything.
4Start the build in a new terminalYou
Cmd+N for a new window, paste the one-line build command once, press Enter.WhyThe build makes lots of files and runs for a while, so your planning terminal stays clean. Pasting the command twice starts two builds.
5Let Claude capture the app footageClaude
Paste the build-terminal message (button at the top). A separate Chrome window opens on GetHookd. Log in once and say "logged in".WhyYou get twice-as-sharp captures, close-ups of just the part that matters, and real typing on screen, without taking a single screenshot. Details at the bottom.
6Approve each still before it movesYou
Look at every Higgsfield still. Say "redo" if anything's off.WhyFixing a still is cheap and a bad clip wastes credits. One of ours had an Apple logo in it.
7Review in the live previewYou
Watch the preview it opens in your browser. Give notes in plain words ("hook faster", "swap the Slack shot").WhyThe preview is instant. A render takes minutes, so save renders for the end.
8Render once, under the capsClaude
Final render at 1080p, 30fps, 4 workers. If it's on pace for more than 10 minutes, stop it and ask what's slow.Why60fps and 4K upscaling multiply render time. Ours took 2 min 38 s at these settings.
9Record the real voiceYou
iPhone Voice Memos, quiet room, one take of the script's lines. Put vo.m4a in ~/Desktop/gethookd-4.0-footage.WhyA human voice lands better on X than the temp AI voice. Claude re-times the whole cut to it.
10Post on launch dayYou
Only when every feature in the video is live. Put the gethookd.ai link in the first reply, not the post.Why"Just dropped" has to be true, and X shows posts with outside links to fewer people.

Why not "computer use"?

On this Mac, Claude's computer use can see Chrome but isn't allowed to click or type in it, and the Claude Chrome extension failed to connect three times before. So we use our browser rig: a separate Chrome that Claude controls (Playwright), built for the TrendTrack crawl (469 pages, zero errors).

Technical steps for the build terminal
  1. Close leftovers: pkill -f "remote-debugging-port=9222"
  2. Launch the rig Chrome with its own profile (Chrome ignores the debug port on the default profile):"/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" --remote-debugging-port=9222 --user-data-dir="$HOME/.lifeos-browser/profile" "https://app.gethookd.ai" >/dev/null 2>&1 &
  3. After "logged in": cd ~/.lifeos-browser && node save-state.mjs, and check that app.gethookd.ai appears.
  4. Write ~/.lifeos-browser/gethookd-capture.mjs on the pattern of agent-lib.mjs (its constants are TrendTrack's, so don't import them): headless Chrome, storageState, viewport 1920x1080, deviceScaleFactor: 2, recordVideo for the Chat and search shots.
  5. Run the shot list. Save to ~/Desktop/gethookd-4.0-footage/auto/: full frames, close-ups and short clips, named by shot number.
  6. If a page redirects to login, stop and ask for a new login.
  7. Make one contact sheet and show it before using anything.
  8. Clean up: pkill -f "remote-debugging-port=9222". While the rig runs, open and AppleScript can hit it instead of Vitaley's own Chrome.

Guardrails
  • Allowed to change anything: up to 3 Chat questions (5.40 to 7.98 credits each), and saving plus tagging one ad for shot 6. Nothing else.
  • Never click: Spy brand (2 credits), Clone, Generate, Save filters, Delete, any settings toggle including Slack, upgrade or checkout.
  • Always crop out: the credits counter, the token and credit line in Chat, and personal folder names.
  • Stop and ask on anything unexpected.

What we learned the hard waySo the next video skips these.

What happenedWhat to do
The first script sold GetHookd itself.A release video introduces what's new. Open on the release and its headline feature.
Screenshots dragged into the chat never saved to disk.Let the corner preview disappear on its own. Screenshots save to ~/Screenshots on this Mac.
4.0 classification wasn't on our account (not in the 22 Explore filters or an ad's Details).Get every launch feature switched on before filming, or use a staging login.
Chat's first answer came back "could not be verified", with its links turned off (1 of 2 tries).Only use verified answers. Launch traffic will ask exactly this question.
Chat costs 5.40 to 7.98 credits a question.Budget it. Starter gets 50 credits a month (landing page draft).
Academy titles include "f*ck it".Crop them out unless Alex signs off.
Our no-em-dash rule vs the app's own text.The rule is for our overlay text only. Real UI stays as it is.
App screens small inside cards were unreadable on a phone.Full-bleed, with a punch-in on the one thing that matters.
