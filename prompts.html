GetHookd prompt library

Type what you sell once. Every prompt below fills itself in. Copy it, paste it into your AI, and you get a ranked answer with a link to every ad it used and one thing to do first.

Before your first prompt
  1. You need the Team or Agency plan. That is where the AI connection lives.
  2. Connect GetHookd to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or Perplexity once. About thirty seconds, in Settings, API & AI Agent.
  3. Each prompt uses a fraction of a credit, well under one. The time label on each prompt is what to expect.
For section 1, type the market or niche you are looking at instead, like "home fitness" or "dog owners".
