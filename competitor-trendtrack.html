The read

TrendTrack sells breadth and freshness across a Meta, TikTok and Google ad corpus, monetises discovery rather than depth, and layers derived signals on top of the raw library. They do not generate anything: no ad images, no scripts. That is our clearest structural advantage. Their edge is packaging and distribution, not product depth. Nearly every gap below is pricing, documentation, or content rather than engineering, which is why most of the recommendations are cheap.

Recommended moves

1
Add quarterly billing
A third cycle between monthly and annual, at a discount below the ~35% annual rate.
Why. Our only step today is monthly to annual: a large discount and a 12-month commitment in one jump. Quarterly gives the hesitant buyer a smaller yes and locks three billing cycles instead of one. Relevant because our own retention data shows first-charge churn is heavily front-loaded.
high impactAlex
2
Confirm before delete on the MCP tools
Add a required confirmation flag to the tools that remove customer data.
Why. Several tools that remove customer data currently execute without a confirmation step. Specific tools and scope are listed in the internal version. TrendTrack requires an explicit confirm flag on theirs.
high impactMladen
3
Add an Enterprise tier
A full-width band beneath the four cards. No price, Contact Sales.
Why. Our ladder stops at $129 and every account that outgrows it has nowhere to go. TrendTrack's own enterprise motion is largely a positioning prop, so the bar to match it is low.
high impactAlex
4
Publish the tool catalogue properly
Document the ~10 genuine data tools with an example prompt and stated return for each, in one place.
Why. Our docs are spread across /mcp/, /api/, a Crisp article and a Scalar-hosted OpenAPI. TrendTrack runs one site listing every tool with an example prompt. Worth noting: 33 of our 44 registered tools are create/read/update/delete variants on 7 features, so the headline number is not the story. The stronger move is composing outcome-shaped tools like their brief_competitor, which returns a full competitor brief in one call where ours would need six.
high impactMladen
5
Fill the changelog
gethookd.ai/whats-new is live and reads "Coming soon" with zero entries. Backfill releases with dates, and show what is coming.
Why. An empty changelog linked in the footer signals a stalled product. TrendTrack publishes 13 dated entries and merchandises them in-app with an unread badge.
high impactVitaley
6
Fix the ad-count contradiction
The homepage says 23 million eight times and 21 Million+ once. /main/ says it ten times, both persona pages nine.
Why. Two different numbers for the same thing on the same page. Pick one, then find-and-replace across roughly nine pages.
medium impactAlex
7
Finish the metering disclosure
Publish the dollar price per credit and the pack sizes.
Why. Most of this is already live: used_credits and remaining_credits in every charged response, the free endpoints named, 402 before any work runs, and 8 credits per brand lookup published on five pages. Only the price and pack sizes are missing.
medium impactAlex
8
Ship a double-sided referral
Customer invites a friend, both sides get a reward.
Why. TrendTrack gives the referrer a free month and the friend 20% off, puts it permanently in the sidebar, and uses it as the sub-headline of the pricing modal so it doubles as a price objection-handler. Our in-app status is unverified.
high impactAlex
9
Add a partner deals marketplace
An in-app page of partner offers.
Why. TrendTrack runs nine vendors under a shared coupon convention. It earns revenue that does not depend on converting more of our own subscriptions.
medium impactAlex
10
Add a glossary
Definition pages for category terms.
Why. TrendTrack runs 59. Cheap page volume that catches searchers at the earliest stage, before they know which product they want.
medium impactMarketing
11
Add a freshness endpoint
An endpoint reporting how current the data is.
Why. TrendTrack claims under 24 hours and backs it with a live endpoint, so a buyer can verify rather than trust. Freshness is a core claim in this category.
medium impactMladen
