Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer to your question?
Get in touch and we’ll answer it.

Why pay for this when Meta’s ad library is free?

Meta’s library is a search box. It cannot sort a niche, rank anything, tell you how long an ad has been running, or compare one brand against its competitors. It shows you ads. It will not tell you which ones have lasted long enough to matter, which angles are crowded, which are wide open, or what to launch next. That is the part we do.

Do you actually have ads in my niche?

We track over 10 million active ads across every ecommerce category, and you can check before you commit. Start the trial, search your niche, and if the coverage is not there, cancel. That is the honest answer, and it is faster than us telling you a number.

How current is the data?

The library refreshes every day and it tracks ads that are live right now, not an archive of things that stopped running two years ago. If a card says 90 days active, it has been running 90 days and it is still running today. Every result links back to the real ad so you can open it and check.

How do I know the numbers are real?

Everything we show is observable in the public ad library: which ads are live, how long each has been running, how many a brand has running, and the actual copy. We do not estimate a brand’s revenue or invent a spend figure, and we do not ask you to take our word for anything. Every answer comes back with the source ad attached.

If I cancel, will I still get charged?

Cancel before your 7 day trial ends and you are not charged, full stop. After that, cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access through the period you already paid for. Refund handling after a renewal has already processed is set by our billing policy, confirm current terms before publishing.

Do I need to be technical to connect my AI agent?

No. You paste one line into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or Perplexity. No code, no developer, no API key to manage, and nothing to install. It takes about thirty seconds. AI agent access is on the Team and Agency plans.

What can I actually ask it?

Anything you would ask a very fast research assistant who has read every ad in your market. Show me trending wellness ads. What angles is my market running that I am not? Which of these has been live the longest? Find brands scaling on Meta this week. Turn this ad into three new statics.

I am just starting out. Is this useful for me?

It is more useful the more you spend, because the whole point is deciding what to test next with real budget behind it. If you are not running ads yet, you will still learn faster by reading what is working than by guessing, but you will get the most out of this once you are shipping creatives every week.

How is this different from Foreplay, AdSpy or TrendTrack?

Those are libraries you scroll. This is a library your AI can read. The difference is not how many ads sit in the database, it is that you can ask a question in plain English and get back the angles your competitors own that you have never run, with the source ads attached.

Can I use it without an AI agent?

Yes. Everything works inside the app on any plan. Explore the library by hand, put a competitor under Brand Spy, dig through Shops, and turn winners into new statics with Clone Ads. The AI connection makes it faster, it is not a requirement.
