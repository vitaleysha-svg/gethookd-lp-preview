The offer

What we are selling

Stop paying to find out an ad does not work.

We read your account against 13 million live Meta ads and tell you what to test next, with the ad already running that proves it.

Why this is the positioning

Price is not the thing we are selling, so it should not be the thing we lead with. A business spending $1M a month does not weigh $499. They weigh what happens to the next $30,000 they put behind a creative. That is the number we move, and it is the only number worth talking about first. Lead with the price and we invite a comparison against a strategist's salary, which is a conversation about cost. Lead with the spend we protect and the price stops being the question.

What they getIn the order they get it.

1The onboarding call60 minutes, once

We go through your account together and leave with one agreed bottleneck and one number we are moving.

  • You walk us through what you sell, who buys it and what you have already tried.
  • We look at your last 30 days together and name the one thing holding the account back.
  • We agree the number we are judging this on, and what good looks like in 30 days.
  • You leave with the export instructions and the Slack invite.
2Your weekly readEvery week, back in 1 to 2 business days

You send one export. We read it against 13 million live Meta ads and hand back a short written call on what to do next.

  • What to kill, and why the numbers say so.
  • What to feed, and how much.
  • What to test next, with the ad already running in your market that proves the angle, its link and how long it has been live.
  • Where your creative sits against what the market is actually running.
3Three statics off a proven winnerWith each weekly read

You do not just get told what to test. You get something to run.

  • We take a winning ad from your market and build three static variants of it for your product.
  • They come with the source ad, so you can see exactly what the angle is doing and why it is working.
  • Static concepts only. Video and UGC are yours to produce.
4Two calls a month30 minutes each

With the strategist who did the read, not a success manager working from notes.

  • Walk through the read and argue with it.
  • Look at anything you launched since the last call.
  • Decide what is being tested before the next one.
5A private Slack channelBetween calls

Same-day answers so you are not sitting on a decision for two weeks.

  • Send a creative before you launch it and get a straight read.
  • Ask for a pull on a competitor or a market any time.
  • Tell us a number moved and we will adjust the plan.
6A monthly open webinarOnce a month

A creative strategist takes questions live, from you and from the rest of the group.

  • Ask anything, including things outside your own account.
  • Watch other operators ask what you had not thought to ask.
7API and MCP accessIncluded, no extra cost

Connect GetHookd to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or Perplexity and ask the library in plain English.

  • Normally Team and Agency only. If you are on a lower plan it comes with this.
  • Comes with the prompt library, so you can run your own pulls between our reads.
8Everything in your GetHookd planUnchanged

This sits on top of what you already pay for. Nothing is taken away or gated.

Not promised, on purpose: the Meta ad-account connection. It is being built and it will make this faster when it lands, but it is not live and it does not go in the offer until it is. If a prospect asks, the honest answer is that today you send the export and it takes five minutes.
