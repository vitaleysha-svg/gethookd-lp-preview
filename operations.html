How the service actually runs

The operating manual behind the offer. Built from the call with Gabriel, Alex's creative strategist. Alex: the things I need from you are in the last section. The one that matters most is you sitting in on the first two or three onboarding calls.

The onboarding call60 minutes. Gabriel's order, research first. Do not diagnose on this call, gather.

1What they sell and to whom10 min

Gabriel's order: research first, production second. Start with the source of truth.

  • Who is the customer you are targeting, and why that person?
  • What is the promise you are making to them?
  • When you decide what ad to make next, what are you actually looking at? Where does that information come from?
  • Gabriel: most people cannot answer the third one, and that alone is the finding.
2Volume and money10 min

Net new concepts per week is the number that matters, not total creatives.

  • How much are you spending a month right now?
  • How many creatives do you test a week, and how many are genuinely new concepts rather than variations?
  • What is your target CPA and ROAS, and how far off are you?
  • Gabriel's own team tests 10 net new concepts a day. Ecommerce is a game of testing.
3What they have already tried15 min

For established brands this is the whole job before you open your mouth.

  • What has worked so far, and what is your best guess why?
  • What have you tested that failed?
  • What do you keep meaning to test and never get to?
  • Gabriel: the biggest letdown from consultants was cookie-cutter advice, because 99% of it was something they had already tested.
4The bottleneck, in their words10 min
  • If you could fix one thing about your ads this month, what would it be?
  • Who makes the creative, and how long does one new concept take you?
  • Which competitors do you watch?
  • How fast can you get a static live once you have it? Added: three a week is gold at 48 hours and a backlog at three weeks.
  • If you wanted to run a test tomorrow, who says yes? Added: if they cannot approve a test, nothing gets tested and the pilot produces no data.
5Close5 min
  • Thirty days from now, what has to have happened for you to say this was worth it? Added: in their words, with a number. Also the best churn question there is.
  • Here is exactly what I need you to send me today.
  • You get the written game plan in one to two days.
  • Then we do this every week, and here is the Slack channel.

Getting their numbersWhat the customer does. They receive this as its own one-pager with screenshots.

1Open Ads Manager and pick the account

adsmanager.facebook.com, then the account picker at the top left.

2Click the Ads tab

One row per ad. Campaigns and Ad sets are too high up to judge creative.

3Set the date range to Last 30 days

Long enough for real spend per ad, recent enough to be the current creative against the current audience. It resets often, check it every time.

4Columns, then Customize columns

Ignore the presets.

5Add the two CTR columns

Searching CTR returns seven results. Only two are the ones we use.

  • CTR (all), every click including expanding the text. Tells us if the hook stops the scroll.
  • CTR (link click-through rate), clicks to the site. Tells us if the copy does its job.
  • Ignore Unique CTR, Outbound CTR, Unique outbound CTR and Marketing messages CTR.
6Add CPC and the three video columns
  • CPC (cost per link click). CPC (all) is harmless to include.
  • 3-second video plays, ThruPlays, Video plays at 75%.
  • Hook rate and hold rate are not columns in Ads Manager. We derive them from those three. They stay blank on image ads, which is normal.
7Add the naming columns

Ad settings tab: Campaign name, Ad set name, Ad ID. Without these we cannot tell a new test from a scaled winner.

8Save it as a preset

The arrow attached to the right of the blue Save button, then Save as new preset. Name it GetHookd weekly. Save on its own remembers nothing.

9Check Breakdown is off

No Popular boxes ticked and Time set to None. A breakdown splits one ad across several rows and makes the file unusable.

10Export as CSV, then repeat for the previous 30 days

Two files. The comparison between them is where the signal is.

The CSV has no creative in it. No images, no video, no copy. Partner access to their Business Manager would fix it but is too big an ask on day one. Instead: Meta Ad Library on every prospect before the call, which shows every ad they have live right now, free and instant. Plus their screenshots of the top 10 by spend and worst 5. The gap that leaves is the creative for ads they already killed, which block 3 of the call covers by asking.

What they get every weekThe artifact. Alex, this is the part worth your eyes most.

The weekly read, one page

  • Headline: the one thing to do this week, in a sentence.
  • What to kill: ad name, spend, the number that condemns it.
  • What to feed: ad name, current spend, suggested spend, why.
  • What to test next: the angle, the live market ad that proves it with its link and run time, and why it applies to them.
  • Your three statics: built off that winner, with the source ad attached.
  • What changed since last week: the number we agreed on, then and now.
Turnaround is one to two business days. It goes in their Slack channel, not email, so the thread stays in one place and they can reply to a specific line.
