What the proposal changesannual stays ten times monthly in every tier

Tier 5 goes from $4.99 to $3.99 (annual $39.90). New tier 6 at $1.99 (annual $19.90). South Africa and Brazil move up to tier 4. Philippines and Turkey move down to tier 5. Nigeria, Pakistan and India move down to tier 6. Tiers 1 to 4 keep their prices. Two facts to hold next to tier 6: Whop's fixed 30 cents per charge is 15 percent of $1.99, and most buyers in those three countries have no card that pays in USD, so the annual pass in local currency is the offer that reaches them.
