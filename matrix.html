03 · What this suggests

The launch gate is the deciding vote. Alex’s message on 26 August was that once both workflows are visible, the test can launch. Only C does that today. If the goal is to start the test this week, C is the only page that qualifies without further work.

B is not wasted. Fourteen of its items are additive rather than conflicting: the demonstration block, the prompt gallery, the Clone Ads section, the objection block, the pricing reframe and the closing line. Every one of those could be folded into C without touching anything Alex asked for. That would produce a page covering roughly 26 of the 29 rows.

Only one row requires a decision rather than work. Row A4, whether library size belongs in the headline. Once that is settled, the rest is build time.
